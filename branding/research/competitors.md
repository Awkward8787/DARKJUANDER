# Competitor scan

Checked October 28, 2026 from each project's own site. Claims below are theirs, quoted or summarized, not verified by us.

| Project | Promise (their words) | Look | Base | Gap DARKJUANDER can own |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kali Linux** (kali.org) | "The most advanced Penetration Testing Distribution" | Blue dragon, dark blue and black, tool-grid screenshots | Debian | A toolbox for attacking things. No story for containing AI agents on your own machine. |
| **Parrot OS** (parrotsec.org) | A "framework for your Cybersecurity operations"; Home, Security, and HTB editions. Site states 50M+ downloads (their figure). | Parrot head, cyan/teal on dark | Debian | Tool-centric and edition-centric. No AI layer or per-agent audit. |
| **Qubes OS** (qubes-os.org) | "A reasonably secure operating system" | Blue cube, plain docs site, endorsements from Edward Snowden and Freedom of the Press Foundation | Xen + Fedora/Debian VMs | Strong isolation, but heavy hardware needs and nothing aimed at AI agents or model routing. |
| **Tails** (tails.net) | Amnesic live system that routes everything through Tor; part of the Tor Project since 2024 | Purple, friendly illustration | Debian | Not a daily workstation by design. No AI layer. |

| **Universal Blue / Bluefin** (universal-blue.org, projectbluefin.io) | Fedora bootc "cloud-native" desktop images, signed with sigstore | Friendly dinosaur, blue | Fedora bootc | Same base technology DARKJUANDER uses, so it is the closest technical neighbor. Developer-desktop focus, no security or AI-agent boundary. |

## What this means for the brand

1. **The category looks the same.** Dark blue, dragons and animals, skulls, tool grids. A violet eclipse and a calm voice stand apart.
2. **Everyone sells tools or compartments. Nobody sells an agent boundary.** That is the line to hold: agents, labels, sandbox, audit log.
3. **Honesty is a differentiator.** Qubes' "reasonably secure" shows that security buyers trust modest, exact claims. DARKJUANDER's README already lists what the demos do not prove. Keep that in every channel.
4. **Avoid Tails purple confusion.** Tails uses a mid purple on light backgrounds. DARKJUANDER's violet sits on near-black and is paired with an eclipse, not a friendly illustration.

Sources: kali.org, parrotsec.org, qubes-os.org, tails.net, whonix.org, blackarch.org/tools.html, universal-blue.org, projectbluefin.io (all opened October 28, 2026).
